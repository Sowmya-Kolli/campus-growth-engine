## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, institutional-yet-accessible aesthetic tailored for forward-thinking university students, campus ambassadors, and academic partners. Striking a deliberate balance between modern enterprise SaaS credibility and high-momentum learning, the visual language communicates clarity, forward progress, and technical rigor without feeling intimidating or cold.

The visual direction merges **Modern SaaS Enterprise** precision with high-conversion product growth patterns:
- **Surface Atmosphere:** Crisp porcelain and slate-white backdrops layered over subtle neutral micro-grids, conveying an organized, structured workspace.
- **Color Dynamism:** High-contrast deep slate and navy text anchors the architecture, paired with purposeful emerald and teal growth accents that celebrate forward momentum, workshop completion, and project execution.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires confidence, executive clarity, curiosity, and rapid execution. Users should feel they are enrolling in a career-defining technical sprint rather than a generic webinar.
- **Tactile Precision:** Restrained 1px structural hairline borders, refined multi-tier diffused ambient shadows, and crisp data micro-badges emphasize enterprise-grade craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate contrast hierarchies designed to maximize conversion readability, trust, and clear affordances.

### Primary & Action Accent Scale
- **Primary Emerald (`#059669`):** Represents growth, active validation, and primary call-to-action triggers (e.g., "Reserve My Seat", "Submit Project").
- **Secondary Deep Teal (`#0d9488`):** Applied to technical highlights, cohort tracks, curriculum tags, and interactive focus states.
- **Tertiary Mint/Teal (`#10b981`):** Reserved for live status indicators, counter badges, verified credential checkmarks, and celebratory progress bars.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Base), complemented by `#F8FAFC` (Slate Canvas / Section Tint) to provide subtle visual pacing across modular dashboard cards.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#FFFFFF` with structural boundary `#E2E8F0` (Hairline Border).
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Anchors page titles, critical data figures, and active button text for maximum readability.
- **Text Secondary (`#334155` - Slate 700):** High-legibility body copy and modular descriptions.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B` - Slate 500):** Captions, timestamps, inactive states, and helper instructions.

### Feedback & Validation
- **Success:** `#059669` surface with `#ECFDF5` background.
- **Warning / Scarcity:** `#D97706` (Amber 600) with `#FFFBEB` background for seat counts under 10% capacity.
- **Critical / Error:** `#E11D48` (Rose 600) with `#FFF1F2` background for invalid student IDs or missed fields.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve consistent geometric rhythm and readability.

- **Display & Large Headlines:** Configured with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700/800) to replicate the authority of high-growth technical infrastructure and software launch platforms.
- **Body & Instructional Copy:** Kept open and readable with proportional line heights (1.5x–1.6x) and natural letter spacing, ensuring lengthy workshop briefs, code breakdowns, and prerequisites remain fatigue-free.
- **Labels, Badges, and Microcopy:** Utilize precise medium-to-bold weights with uppercase badge conversions to ground metric tags, timing countdowns, and real-time live availability labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an 8pt modular grid aligned with a responsive 12-column system for wide-screen enterprise dashboards and high-conversion landing flows.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** Max-width container capped at `1200px` for optimal form conversion focus. 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) outer canvas margins. Sticky sidebars host real-time seat availability cards alongside syllabus details.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters. Registration forms collapse into a full-width bottom sheet or dedicated lower-section dock.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Action buttons stretch to `100%` container width with a sticky bottom registration bar.

### Spacing Density Principles
- Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop and steps down to `space-md` (`1rem`) on small viewports.
- Form field stacks and input groups strictly employ `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) between label, control, and micro-copy validation errors.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **multi-layered ambient depth** paired with structural hairline borders rather than aggressive heavy drops. This keeps the workspace crisp and data-dense.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Flat / Base Layer (`elevation-0`):** `#F8FAFC` background canvas with zero shadow. Used for page grounding and non-interactive layout containers.
2. **Card Rest / Level 1 (`elevation-1`):**
   - Hairline border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
   - Used for content modules, syllabus cards, and static registration steps.
3. **Interactive Hover / Level 2 (`elevation-2`):**
   - Hairline border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
   - Transition: `box-shadow 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), border-color 200ms ease`
   - Used for interactive cohort selection, hoverable workshop cards, and active form panels.
4. **Overlay / Level 3 (`elevation-3`):**
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`
   - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - Used for sticky quick-register bottom bars, live session modal dialogs, and validation drawer states.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a modern geometry of **rounded level 2 (0.5rem base)**, reinforcing high-utility software discipline without visual harshness.

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Standard for form inputs, primary CTA buttons, interactive list items, and segment pickers.
- **Surface Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard for content cards, workshop modules, modal dialogs, and registration wrappers.
- **Badge Radius (`rounded-full` / Pill):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, live countdown tags, university partner logos, and progress chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#059669` emerald fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border radius, `0 1px 2px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)` shadow. On hover, background shifts to `#047857` with slight elevation (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.3)`). Active state applies a subtle `scale(0.985)` transform.
- **Secondary Action:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. On hover: border transitions to `#CBD5E1` with `#F8FAFC` background.
- **Focus Rings:** Dual ring with `2px` offset (`#FFFFFF`) and `2px` primary emerald indicator (`#059669`).

### Inputs & Registration Form Fields
- **Container:** `44px` minimum touch height, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, typography `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#0D9488` with a diffused glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`.
- **Validation Error:** Border `1px solid #E11D48`, error text in `label-sm` with an alert icon offset by `space-xs`.
- **Pre-filled / Verified:** Suffix icon featuring emerald checkmark in `#ECFDF5` circular badge.

### Status Chips & Data Badges
- **Live Cohort Status:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` label, featuring a pulsing `6px` emerald circular node (`#10B981`) indicating active real-time seats.
- **Curriculum Duration Tag:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` label, uppercase `badge-caption` typography with `0.06em` letter spacing.

### Cards & Syllabus Modules
- **Base Surface:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Active / Milestone Card:** Accent left border `4px solid #059669` to highlight the current live workshop step or project milestone within the 60-minute agenda.

### Radio & Checkbox Selectors
- **Selection Card Group:** Custom clickable block cards with radio inputs embedded. Unselected: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Selected: `2px solid #059669` with background `#F0FDF4`, displaying active student track confirmation.
